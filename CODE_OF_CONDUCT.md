# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming, respectful community for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive and patient.
- Discuss evidence and ideas; accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Respect different viewpoints, personal boundaries and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. These standards apply in project issues, pull requests and community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, request a private maintainer contact in a GitHub issue without naming people or posting sensitive details. Share the report only after a private channel is arranged. Maintainers will respect the privacy of reporters and may remove content, limit participation or ban participants according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.
